About
// 03 — About

On-Set to In-Engine

I'm a cinematics and photography-focused artist with 3+ years across 20+ feature film and episodic VFX productions. I shoot on a Fujifilm X-T4 and apply the same decisions, focal length, aperture, timing, to virtual cameras in Unreal Engine 5.

My background is 3D scanning: I built and ran capture pipelines on set at Industrial Pixel 3D Studios. That on-set experience is what informs how I light, frame, and compose in-engine. Scanning is the foundation; cinematics is where I'm going.
