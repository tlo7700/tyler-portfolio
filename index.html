On-Set to In-Engine

Lead 3D Scanning Technician with 3+ years across 20+ feature film and episodic VFX productions. On set I problem-solve hardware, optimize capture pipelines under live production pressure, and carry that data through into production-ready assets.

I operate multi-camera synchronized rigs, FARO LiDAR systems, and HDRI capture workflows on set at Industrial Pixel 3D Studios, Vancouver.

I also build the pipeline tools: Python automation, Google Apps Script, and Raspberry Pi hardware integration that keep scan teams moving fast in high-pressure production environments.

Building toward technical cinematics and real-time production, with active UE5 environment and cinematic work alongside established scan-to-engine pipelines.
