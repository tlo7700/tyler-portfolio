// Overview

A self-directed cinematic environment piece built around a single mythic image: a glowing sword, rune-etched and wreathed in flame, waiting to be claimed.

The project explores fantasy world-building through lighting, shading, and atmosphere — using two distinct cinematic shots to tell the same story from different emotional distances.

Shot 01 — The Object.  A slow dolly pull opens on a tight close-up of the blade, letting the shimmering light and glowing orange runes establish a sense of ancient, mysterious power before pulling back to reveal the full subject in its environment.

Shot 02 — The Encounter.  The same dolly movement, reframed. A lone figure steps into frame mid-pull, gazing up at the sword — shifting the scene from object study to character moment, and giving the environment stakes.

Key Challenge

Designing lighting and shading that could hold up at extreme close range while still reading clearly in a wide environment shot — two very different demands on the same set of assets.
