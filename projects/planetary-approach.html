Engine Unreal Engine 5
Type Real-Time Planetary Cinematic
Discipline Environment Art · Lighting · Cinematics
// Overview

A two-shot real-time cinematic built entirely in Unreal Engine 5, designed to demonstrate shot-to-shot lighting continuity, large-scale environment composition, and character integration in a live engine context.

The environment is two shots, a gas giant and an astronaut adrift in its shadow. Rather than building tension through action, the scale does the work. The planet fills the frame not as a backdrop but as a presence, something indifferent and enormous. The astronaut isn't moving toward anything. He's just there, small against something that doesn't register him at all. The silence isn't empty. It has weight.

The lighting language had to hold across both cuts. The gas giant doubles as the key light source, a green-tinted rim wrapping the astronaut suit from behind, keeping the character readable against the planet surface without breaking the mood. That continuity was the primary technical challenge: making a single environmental light source feel consistent across a cut to a completely different composition.

The planet material was built procedurally in Blender using noise and color ramp nodes to generate the band structure, then baked and brought into UE5 as a texture applied to a subdivided cube mesh, with UV layouts matched by exporting the same mesh used for baking. Atmospheric glow at the planet limb is a Fresnel-driven emissive in the UE5 material graph.

Focus

Maintaining lighting continuity across a cut, using a single planetary light source to key and rim the astronaut across two compositions without breaking the silence of the scene.

// Role

Sole author: environment art, planet shading, lighting, camera animation, and sequence editing.
